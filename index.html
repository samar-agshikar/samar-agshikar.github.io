Why I Build

From Coswig to Ahmedabad—learning to build where I am.

Between August 2023 and June 2025, I studied at Gymnasium Coswig in Germany. Moving to Ahmedabad, India in July 2025 meant adapting to a completely new academic rhythm, culture, and peer group.

Experiencing two different education systems up close showed me a universal problem: students spend more energy organizing scattered tools than actually mastering ideas. Instead of waiting for someone else to fix it, I started shipping software—launching College Chances India, piloting THE 1 inside my own school community, and building Kerniq.

  • School Pilot (10/2026): Tested directly with students during THE 1’s school pilot at Adani International School.
  • Product Hunt Launch (10/2026): Shipped publicly on Product Hunt after iterating on student feedback.
  • Core Philosophy: Keep essential student productivity and AI study tools 100% free and accessible.
Engineering Philosophy

How I build software.

Great student software shouldn’t feel bloated or intimidating. I focus on clean interfaces, fast feedback loops, and AI that strengthens human thinking rather than replacing it.

CORE STACK & PRINCIPLES HTML · CSS · JavaScript · AI Systems · Rapid Iteration

1. Connected Student Workflows

Designing timetables, notes, homework, and revision in one coherent environment so context is never lost between tabs.

2. Pedagogical AI Assistance

Building AI inside THE 1 and Kerniq that breaks down complex concepts into structured quizzes and guided exploration.

3. Classroom-First Validation

Testing features with real classmates—like THE 1’s pilot at Adani International School—before scaling publicly.
