Ventures & Projects

In Active Development Founder & Builder

KerniqOS

A long-term venture to build a personalized, cross-platform developer ecosystem.

kerniq@the-1.app

Vision & Problem

Building software across devices and platforms remains fragmented. KerniqOS aims to make cross-platform development significantly more accessible, intelligent, and cohesive.

Current Direction

Development is currently focused on a cross-platform developer desktop application, establishing core workflows and architecture.

Explorations & Long-Term Roadmap

Concepts actively being explored for future iterations (not yet released):

  • Intelligent, AI-assisted development environments
  • Device-aware & cross-platform development workflows
  • Integrated templates, developer resources & personalized workspaces
  • Hand-gesture interaction and novel desktop computing experiences
  • Long-term expansion toward a proprietary Linux-based operating system
Launched · Piloted Founder & Developer

THE 1

An all-in-one student workspace unifying fragmented academic tools.

Visit the-1.app ↗

Purpose & Product

Students routinely juggle disconnected apps for schedules, notes, revision, and assignments. THE 1 consolidates core academic workflows into a single, accessible workspace designed to improve how students organize and study.

Traction & Validation

Developed through real product iterations and direct student feedback, including a school pilot at Adani International School and a public launch on Product Hunt.
