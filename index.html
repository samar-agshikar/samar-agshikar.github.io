Builder Philosophy

How I build & learn.

You do not need to wait until university or adulthood to start building meaningful things. Every project is a cycle of curiosity, execution, and real-world feedback.

01 — Curiosity & First Principles
02 — Prototyping & Experiments
03 — Real Users & Iteration
04 — Long-Term Ambition
01 / CURIOSITY

Curiosity & First Principles

Whether studying mathematics, exploring operating systems, or thinking about aviation, I start by understanding how complex systems actually work—then asking how software can make them more accessible or intuitive.

02 / EXPERIMENTATION

Prototyping & Experiments

Beyond main ventures, I regularly build experimental prototypes—from macOS notch interaction concepts and Python/OpenCV privacy tools (PrivacyShield) to tablet interface explorations (SamarOS)—to test ideas hands-on.

03 / ITERATION

Real Users & Iteration

Shipping THE 1 on Product Hunt, running a student pilot at Adani International School, and learning from College Chances India taught me that real user feedback matters far more than assumptions.

04 / EXECUTION

Long-Term Ambition

My current execution objective is to ship a genuinely usable, polished KerniqOS product by 31 December 2026 and pursue opportunities like Y Combinator—while continuing to grow academically and as a student leader.
