About

Curiosity grounded in execution.

I am a Grade 8 student at Adani International School (ADIS) in Ahmedabad, India. Prior to moving to India in July 2025, I completed Grades 5 and 6 at Gymnasium Coswig in Germany, shaping an international perspective across both German and Indian education systems.

I believe you do not need to wait until university or adulthood to start building meaningful software. My work focuses on three pillars: academic excellence, technical engineering capability, and leadership through real contribution.

Whether designing developer tools, shipping student workspaces, or studying mathematics and aviation, I approach problems from first principles—prototyping early, listening to real users, and iterating with discipline.
